## Vision

### The premise that was challenged

The question brought to review was "is MindKshetra a Gita app or an astrology
app?" — because astrology is 8,904 LOC (42% of a 21.4k LOC codebase) and appears
nowhere in the product's positioning line.

That framing was rejected. Evidence: a grep across `lib/astrology/`,
`app/api/astrology/`, `app/astrology/`, and `components/astrology/` for any
reference to a sloka, verse, or the Gita returns **zero hits**. The reverse grep
— `/api/chat` and `lib/retrieve.ts` for chart, member, or astro — also returns
**zero hits**. `lib/astrology/blend.ts` blends astrological *systems* (Parashari
houses vs KP significators vs dasha timing), not Gita and Jyotish.

The two halves share exactly six infrastructure imports (`LanguageProvider`,
`AuthProvider`, `lib/groq.ts`, `lib/rateLimit.ts`, `lib/redis.ts`,
`lib/supabase/server`) and **zero domain imports**.

So the real problem is not identity. It is:

> Two chat products live in one binary and never speak to each other, and the
> thing that would make either one defensible is the connection that was never
> built.

### 10x check

Today's astrology market answers *"what will happen to me?"* — a question that
makes the user passive, and one AstroTalk answers with 15,000 human consultants
that cannot be out-hired.

The two halves together can answer a question nobody in that market can:
*"this is the pressure in your chart, and here is what Krishna told Arjuna about
meeting exactly this."* The chart supplies specificity; the Gita supplies agency.
That flips astrology from fatalism to practice.

### Landscape evidence

| Market | Numbers |
|---|---|
| Gita apps | 82 active competitors, exactly 1 ever funded. No monetization in evidence. |
| Astrology apps | $5.69B (2026) → $11.71B (2030), 19.8% CAGR. Vedic = 60%+ of India. |
| AstroTalk | $43M ARR, +25% YoY, ~$350M valuation, 35M+ users, ₹15-50/min human consults. |
| AstroSage | 1.5M DAU, 12M MAU, 70M+ downloads, ~80% kundli market share. |

The dominant 2026 criticism of AI astrology apps is that they **skip the real
math** — birth date into an LLM trained on internet horoscope content, producing
readings that "sound good but you could find on any horoscope website." Co-Star
is named for readings generic enough to fit any chart. A category review states
the bar plainly: *"the best AI astrology app in 2026 is one that computes a real
Swiss Ephemeris natal chart and then uses a large language model to interpret it
in plain language."*

MindKshetra already clears that bar: `sweph@2.10.3-7` with checked-in `.se1`
ephemeris files, plus 18 focused classical modules (`vargas`, `dasha`, `yogas`,
`dignities`, `kp`, `lalkitab`, `panchang`, `transits`, `aspects`).

### Eureka — where conventional wisdom is wrong here

Naive focus-as-subtraction says: positioning says Gita, 42% of code says
astrology, cut the astrology. That is backwards. **The positioning is wrong, not
the code.** Subtraction here would delete the only half with a proven $5.69B
willingness-to-pay and keep the half that is a beautiful, unmonetizable
commodity.

And the reverse holds, which is what makes this a moat rather than a pivot: every
astrology app is transactional — pay per minute, get a prediction, leave. None
have a soul layer. **Nobody in the astrology market has the Gita, and nobody in
the Gita market has real ephemeris math.** The intersection is the moat.

## 🔴 P0 — live production safety gap (D6)

`detectCrisis` is imported and called in exactly one place:
`app/api/chat/route.ts:96`. It is **not** called in
`app/api/astrology/chat/route.ts`.

The astrology chat's system prompt (`lib/astrology/predictions.ts:482`) instead
contains: `- If the question is off-chart, briefly decline and steer back.`

```
  /api/chat (Madhav)                      /api/astrology/chat
  ──────────────────                      ───────────────────
  detectCrisis(lastUser.content)          (no crisis check exists)
    ↓ detected                                 ↓
  console.warn("[chat] crisis...")        streams Groq reply
    ↓                                          ↓
  crisisResponse(language)                prompt says "off-chart → decline,
    ↓                                     steer back" to the chart
  iCall 9152987821                             ↓
  Vandrevala 1860-2662-345                user in crisis is redirected
  Emergency 112                           to their 8th house.
  "seek human help first"                 No helpline. No warning logged.
```

Silent to the system (no log), silent to the operator (no alert), silent to the
user (fluent chart-cited reply instead of a phone number). All helpline copy
already exists in `lib/crisis.ts` in both EN and HI; the disclaimer string
exists at `lib/i18n/dictionary.ts:175`. None of it is wired to this route.

Approach A makes this **heavier**: a chart-grounded reply carries more authority.
"Saturn is transiting your 8th house and this period is difficult" said to a
suicidal person is materially more dangerous than the same sentence without
astrological backing.

**Decision: fix now, as its own commit, before any integration work.** Import,
call, warn, return — using code the Gita route already exercises. Deeper
persona-level safety design for chart-grounded advice rides along with the
integration.

### Why two phases (D7.final)

The accepted set contains two independent bets. Bet 1: does chart-grounded
scripture feel different enough that people return. Bet 2: will people pay for
kundli milan. Both are sound. Shipped simultaneously they produce **one muddy
signal** — a disappointing retention number could not be attributed to the
thesis or to paywall placement, and the paywall becomes a suspect variable in
judging whether the integration worked at all.

Nothing was cut. Sequencing only. Same total scope, two clean reads instead of one.

### Thesis correction (supersedes the "Vision" framing)

The vision section above frames **chart→verse retrieval** as the moat. That is
mechanically wrong and the review verified it:

```
  retrieveSlokas() score composition  (lib/retrieve.ts:216-236)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  vector similarity  × 0.7 × 10   ──▶  up to ~7.0   ← driven by the user's WORDS
  token overlap      +1.8/token   ──┐
  tag-name overlap   +2.0/token   ──┼─ inside tagScoreSlokas, then × 0.3
  tag match  weight×(0.5+rarity)  ──┘  ← chart tags land HERE, max ≈ 0.57
```

A chart-derived tag contributes **at most ~0.57** where the vector arm alone reaches
**~7.0**. And `LIFE_AREAS` has exactly six entries (`lib/astrology/blend.ts:14-21`) at
2-3 tags each, so every career-pressured user receives the identical three tags —
**coarser than the 18 moods the app already exposes**.

**Corrected thesis:** the differentiator is the **chart-reading voice**, which uses real
`AreaFact` detail, dasha windows and house placements. The chart→verse bridge is a
**supporting signal**, not the moat. This also makes E6's match-reasoning line honest.

## Design Review Decisions (2026-07-26)

**3/10 → 7/10**. Ten decisions made, four items still open. **Two of these supersede

### Two structural rules

1. **Voice labels are UI chrome the app renders, never text the model emits.**
   `lib/groq.ts:90` states *"NEVER use section labels or report-style headings"* with a
   forbidden-examples list. Rendering labels in the interface respects that rule, keeps
   each prompt single-purpose, and makes the **G1 empty-voice state structurally
   impossible** — a voice with no content simply never renders its label. G1 is closed.
2. **The chart epigraph slot is height-reserved**, so two parallel streams can never
   reflow text the user is mid-sentence on.
