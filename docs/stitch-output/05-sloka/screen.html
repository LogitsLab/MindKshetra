BG 2.47
CHAPTER 02 · VERSE 47

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥

karmaṇy-evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana ।
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo ’stv akarmaṇi ॥

तेरा कर्म करने में ही अधिकार है, उसके फलों में कभी नहीं। तू कर्मों के फल का हेतु मत हो और तेरी अकर्म में भी आसक्ति न हो।

‘You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions.’

Reflection

This verse calls us to embrace the purity of action. When we release our attachment to outcomes, we find freedom from anxiety. True spiritual mastery lies not in avoiding responsibility, but in performing it as a sacred offering, unburdened by the ego's desire for reward.
