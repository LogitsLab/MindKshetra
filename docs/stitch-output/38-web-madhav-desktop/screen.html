"What does my current Saturn placement mean for my career path?"

Saturn holds your tenth house until March 2028.

In the Vedic tradition, Saturn's transit through the tenth house of Karma is not a period of restriction, but of refinement. You are being asked to build structures that will outlast your current ambitions.

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन | — BHAGAVAD GITA 2.47

Focus on the duty itself, rather than the immediate rewards. The 'Void' you feel is simply the space between who you were and the leader you are becoming. Ground your daily practice in silent meditation before the sun peaks.
