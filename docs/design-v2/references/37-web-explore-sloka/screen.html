Bhagavad Gita

18 Chapters · 700 Verses
Chapter 02 · Verse 47

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥ ४७ ॥

karmaṇy-evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana |
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr mā te saṅgo ’stv akarmaṇi

Translation

You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions. Never consider yourself to be the cause of the results of your activities, nor be attached to inaction.

आपका अधिकार केवल कर्म करने में ही है, उसके फलों में कभी नहीं। इसलिए आप कर्मों के फल हेतु मत बनिए और न ही आपकी आसक्ति अकर्म में हो।

lightbulb

Wisdom Reflection

This verse defines the essence of Nishkama Karma—selfless action. Focus on the process, the effort, and the purity of intent. By detaching from the outcome, you liberate yourself from the anxiety of expectation, allowing your soul to perform at its highest, most divine capacity.
