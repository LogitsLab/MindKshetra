What does my current Saturn placement mean for my career?

Response

"Saturn holds your tenth house until March 2028."

auto_awesome

In the Vedic tradition, Saturn (Shani) represents the principle of Tapas—discipline and the burning away of the non-essential. Placing it in your tenth house of career suggests a period of karmic consolidation.

The Gita teaches us in Chapter 2, Verse 47: Karmanye vadhikaraste ma phaleshu kadachana. You have a right to your duty, but not to its results. This Saturn transit is not a delay, but an invitation to focus purely on the excellence of your action (Dharma) without the anxiety of immediate recognition.
