# CSR & grants — one-pager

Can institutional money supplement dāna without bending the product? Short
answer: yes, but only after entity registration, and only from funders who
accept the product as-is. This page is the decision reference; the money
rails themselves live in [nonprofit-kickoff.md](nonprofit-kickoff.md).

## Indian CSR (Companies Act §135)

Companies above the thresholds must spend 2% of net profit on Schedule VII
activities. MindKshetra plausibly fits **mental wellbeing / preventive
healthcare** and **education** framings — pitch the practice layer and
student programs (campus packs), not astrology.

Prerequisites, in order — all owner-gated:

1. Entity: Section 8 company or registered trust/society.
2. 12A + 80G registrations (tax exemption; donor deductibility).
3. **CSR-1 registration** with MCA — without it no company can route CSR
   funds here.
4. Then approach company foundations (Indian IT/finance houses run
   wellbeing programs).

Realistic timeline: entity + registrations are months, not weeks. Nothing
blocks product work meanwhile.

## Grants

- **Fits**: mental-wellbeing program grants, open-source infrastructure
  funds (the AGPL codebase is a credential), Indic-language/digital-public-
  goods programs (Hindi-first, free forever).
- **Poor fits**: anything requiring clinical claims (we make none), or
  funders uncomfortable with scripture or astrology in the same product —
  do not contort the product to pass a screen.
- **FCRA rule (hard)**: foreign grant money requires FCRA registration —
  years away, if ever. Until then international support flows only through
  the fiscal-host rail (Open Collective), never mixed with the Indian
  entity's books.

## Guardrails

- Institutional money buys infrastructure and student access, never
  features — the launch gates and free-forever posture are not negotiable
  per funder.
- One funder should never exceed ~a third of the budget; dāna stays the
  base so no funder owns the roadmap.
- Every grant gets a line in the public transparency table.
