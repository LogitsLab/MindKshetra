# Competitor register

Who could attack this position, what they would ship, and what survives the
attack. Reviewed with the strategy docs; update when a named player moves.
(Landscape facts from the 2026-07 reviews: ~82 active Gita apps with almost
no funding among them; AstroTalk-class consult marketplaces at $40M+ ARR;
Medito and Smiling Mind sustaining the free-forever nonprofit model.)

## The three attackers that matter

### 1. An astrology marketplace adds devotional content (AstroTalk-class)

The most resourced attacker. They have distribution, Hindi-first users, and
paid-consult revenue to fund it. Their version of "Gita + Jyotish" would be
content marketing wrapped around the consult funnel — verses as engagement,
astrologers as the product.

**What they cannot copy:** the free-forever nonprofit posture (their unit
economics forbid it), the no-consult trust position ("nobody here is
selling you a remedy"), and the deterministic chart→verse editorial bridge
(they monetize uncertainty; we bridge it to a teaching).

### 2. A funded Gita/devotional app bolts on an astrology API

Cheaper attack: license a panchang/kundli API, ship "your chart" as a tab.
Plausible for any of the venture-adjacent devotional apps.

**What they cannot copy quickly:** real ephemeris math in-house (they rent
it), the verified 26-tag verse vocabulary and mood graph built over the
whole corpus, crisis-safety plumbing, and an AGPL codebase self-hosters
already trust. An API-rented chart with no editorial bridge is a widget,
not a practice.

### 3. A commercial wellness app ships a "Gita pack" (Calm/Headspace-class)

They own the meditation-habit market and could commission Gita-themed
content in a quarter. Their Hindi/India localization keeps improving.

**What they cannot copy:** scripture as the spine rather than a content
pack (practice here always hangs off a verse), the sangha model (circles,
not a content library), and the price (free forever vs subscription).

## Surviving assets (rank-ordered)

1. **Community trust** — nonprofit, no paywall, no consult upsell, AGPL.
2. **The editorial bridge** — chart fact → verse → action, deterministic
   and provenance-honest. Data plus judgment, accumulated in the repo.
3. **Hindi-first practice surfaces** — authored EN+HI, not translated after.
4. **Crisis-safety spine** — helpline-first handling a growth team would
   deprioritize.
5. **Swiss Ephemeris in-house** — engine, panchang, festivals with golden
   tests; no API dependency to squeeze.

## What we deliberately do not defend

Paid consults (never building them), generic meditation content, horoscope
notifications-as-engagement. If an attacker wins those, nothing here is
lost.
